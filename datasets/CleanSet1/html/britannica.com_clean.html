The Mighty Mushroom

Today is National Mushroom Day in the United States, a celebration of the fungal fruiting body in all its forms and functions, including their culinary charms and environmental importance. The humble mushroom is in fact more powerful than it may appear—some of the largest (and among the oldest) organisms on Earth are mushroom-producing fungi. There are species capable of surviving in space, others that can glow in the dark, and still others that can eat plastic. Some are delicious, some deadly, but all of them are complex, more closely related to animals than to plants. So let’s take a closer look at something we often overlook: the mighty, magnificent mushroom.

News and Notes

In the past week violence broke out between Pakistan and Afghanistan, and an African president was pushed out in a coup.

Afghanistan-Pakistan conflict

On Thursday, October 9, following an attack a day earlier in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province by the Tehreek-e Taliban Pakistan (TTP, sometimes called Pakistani Taliban) on Pakistani soldiers, Pakistan carried out an air strike in Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan, that targeted Noor Wali Mehsud, the leader of the TTP. Days later, Afghanistan carried out retaliatory operations that killed at least 23 Pakistani soldiers and left at least 9 Afghan soldiers dead, although both sides claimed to have inflicted higher casualties. The skirmishes threatened to escalate into a broader conflict, but the hostilities subsided on Monday.

Madagascar’s president flees

Andry Rajoelina, who has served three terms as Madagascar’s president (2009–14, 2019–23, and 2023–25), fled the country in fear of his life earlier this week, pushed out by a coup similar to the one that first swept him into power. Rajoelina (pictured below) has faced significant unrest since late September, led by the country’s Generation Z cohort, who demonstrated against the country’s faltering quality of life, including its chronic shortages of power and water. The balance of power abruptly shifted on Sunday, when the military announced that it was siding with the protesters. Yesterday the National Assembly voted to impeach Rajoelina and Col. Michael Randrianirina declared that the military had taken power.
