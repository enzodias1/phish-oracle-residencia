Advanced DNS Management with Full Control

Get full DNS control with advanced management, real-time analytics, traffic optimization, and enterprise-grade security.

Edge DNS & Redundancy (Global Anycast+ Network for Reliability)

What It Does:

  • Our Full-Service Edge DNS ensures maximum uptime with an authoritative DNS network (AS16552).
  • Built on the Triple IP Anycast+ (TIPA+) edge network, providing unparalleled reliability.
  • Fully redundant DNS setup prevents outages and ensures seamless domain resolution.

Why It Matters:

  • Zero downtime with multi-tiered redundancy.
  • 100% uptime guarantee for 14+ years.
  • Seamless secondary DNS integration.
Advanced Analytics (Real-Time Traffic Monitoring & Logging)

What It Does:

  • Get real-time insights into DNS queries and traffic flow.
  • Proprietary logging services provide a deeper understanding of traffic sources and potential threats.
  • Predict and troubleshoot DNS attacks with Real-Time Query Analytics.

Why It Matters:

  • Gain full visibility into DNS performance.
  • Identify & prevent potential threats before they escalate.
  • Make data-driven optimizations for improved resolution speeds.
Traffic Management (Failover, Global Traffic Director, ANAME Records)

What It Does:

  • DNS Failover automatically redirects traffic during outages.
  • Global Traffic Director (GTD) optimizes query resolution based on location.
  • ANAME Records, created by DNS Made Easy, allow FQDN mapping at the root domain.

Why It Matters:

  • Eliminates downtime with automatic failover.
  • Ensures faster load times with global routing.
  • Optimizes traffic flow for seamless user experience.
Security & Multi-User Management (SAML/SSO, Granular Permissions)

What It Does:

  • Granular access control lets you assign permissions for records, domains, billing, and Sonar management.
  • Full SAML / SSO integration for corporate and enterprise security.
  • Advanced firewalls & DDoS mitigation strategies prevent unauthorized access.

Why It Matters:

  • Ensures secure, role-based access.
  • Prevents DNS hijacking & unauthorized changes.
  • Supports enterprise security standards with SOC 2 compliance.
Automation & API Support (REST API & SDK for Easy Scaling)

What It Does:

  • Full REST API & SDK support enables seamless automation.
  • Easily provision subdomains, manage clients’ domains, and integrate with existing systems.
  • Built for DevOps teams needing advanced scripting and automation capabilities.

Why It Matters:

  • Eliminates manual configurations with automated updates.
  • Scales effortlessly for large and growing businesses.
  • Supports third-party integrations for streamlined workflows.
